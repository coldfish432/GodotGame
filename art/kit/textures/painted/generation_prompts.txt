# City texture generation prompts

Exact image-generation prompts for the nine source paintings and the two
targeted source edits. The final PNGs also go through
`build_city_textures.py` for size, palette and seam processing.

## C1 city_road.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: hand-painted source for a seamless game terrain texture, C1 old asphalt road in ruined Lungmen lower city. Create ONE square, straight overhead orthographic, evenly lit pixel-art surface swatch. Broad worn charcoal asphalt planes with branching thin hard-edged cracks, irregular repaired asphalt patches, sparse dark oil stains, a few small pothole pools and tiny gravel clusters. No lane markings, no curb, no sidewalk, no objects, no shadow, no directional light, no highlights, no perspective. Natural nonrepeating distribution; detail must remain legible when reduced to 256x256 and tiled. Four sides should continue seamlessly. Use ONLY approximate palette #22262a #2f3438 #454e54 with restrained #3e2a1c #5a3420 for dirt and #5c6064 for sparse aggregate, roughly 8-12 discrete colors. Hard pixel-cluster edges, opaque, no blur or soft glow. This must read darker than concrete pavement.
```

## C2 pavement source edit

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the supplied square pavement texture source for a seamless 3m x 3m game tile. Change ONLY the conspicuous large brown missing corner at the middle-right slab (approximately x=840-940, y=420-545): repair most of it with matching light gray weathered concrete while keeping a much smaller subtle brown chipped corner at the joint. Preserve the exact 6 by 6 slab grid, all other cracks, small chips, grass tufts, flat neutral palette, straight top-down view, overall size and painted hard-edged style. The texture must remain repeatable at all four edges. No new focal mark, shadow, lighting, blur, text or perspective.
```

## C5 roof source edit

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the supplied flat-roof seamless texture source only to reduce obvious repeating focal details. Keep the same square top-down painted roof, all bitumen seams, patches, damp areas, moss, color, scale and four matching edges. Make the two drain grates at upper left and lower right about half their present visual size and much lower contrast: small flush dark-gray square outlets with no bright frame or rust halo. Soften the single distinctive large outlined L-shaped patch in the lower left into several less regular low-contrast tar repairs. The roof should read as quiet neutral material when repeated 3x3. No new drain, spotlight, perspective, cast shadow, windows, text, blur or illumination.
```
