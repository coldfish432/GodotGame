### 6. `family_eraser` · Family Eraser

PRTS: a killer loyal to the family who comes and goes in shadow; everyone knows his title and nobody speaks of him. He is hidden whenever nobody is engaging him.

- **Look:** tall and thin. **A white oval mask** (`#eeece6`) with two black eye holes and a band of 4–5 dark vertical bars across the brow (PRTS has a barcode there: plain bars, nothing readable); pointed ears; **a long dark olive trench coat** (`#45453c` / `#3f3f3a`), white shirt, **a purple tie** (`#6c4a7a`, not a family colour); a short curved blade in the right hand; two throwing blades between the fingers of the left.
- **The mask is what the player sees first** when he appears, so it must be the brightest mass on him and readable on its own.
- **Stealth is done in code** (transparency and a ripple shader). **Do not draw a faded or invisible version.**
- **Rows:**
  - IDLE: standing very still, blade low.
  - START (appearing, 0.35 s): a sudden turn to face the heading, the coat flaring. This is the frame the game fades in from, mask first.
  - CHARGE: very low crouch, the curved blade reversed and held behind the back, **mask turned straight along the heading**.
  - STRIKE: a long, low lunging slash along the heading.
  - RECOVER: rising from the lunge, blade out to the side.
  - **6th row, THROW:** a flick of the left wrist across the body; two blades leave the fingers. Draw the hand empty after the throw; the game draws the blades in flight.
- Walk: a quick, quiet glide, body leaning forward, coat trailing.
