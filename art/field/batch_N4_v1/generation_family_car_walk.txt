### 7. `family_car` · Family Car

PRTS: the family's most common vehicle. The inside is as plain as the outside; the only reinforced part is the bumper. They also use it to see their enemies off on their last ride.

- **In game it is a roadblock, not a cruising car:** it stays parked in a gap at the mouth of a street leading out of a piazza, nose toward the piazza, with a row of barrels along each side. It lurches out up to 4 m when the player stands in front, then reverses back into its slot (the walk board played backwards; no extra frames). Players must destroy it to go on.
- **Look:** a black 1940s sedan: rounded roof and body, separate rounded front wings, two round headlamps, a vertical-slat grille, running boards. Body `#2b2f38` (black with a little blue-grey), **a thick chrome reinforced bumper** (`#c9c9c9`, much brighter than the rest: it is the car's accent and its weapon), a blank pale licence plate, dark windows (no one visible inside).
- **Size:** 24 baked px tall seen from the S (front, from above), the same whole-car box in every column. **In W and E (profile) it is about 52 px long and must stay inside the 64 px cell**; if it would not, angle it slightly rather than shrinking it.
- **Directions** are the same 8 columns, so the car must be drawn turned to all 8 headings: S shows the grille and both headlamps, N the boot and rear lamps, W/E a true profile.
- **Action rows:**
  - IDLE: parked, engine idling, lamps dark. **This is the pose the player sees most** (it is a roadblock), so the S column (nose to camera) must read instantly as a car blocking the way.
  - START: **both headlamps light** (each a bright spot of at least 3×3 px after baking).
  - CHARGE: revving: the rear squats down, the nose lifts, a puff of tyre smoke at the rear wheels.
  - STRIKE: lurching forward hard: the body low and level, front wheels straight. The game holds this frame for the whole (short) ram.
  - RECOVER: braking hard: the nose dips, a wisp of white smoke from the bonnet.
  - **6th row, WRECK:** destroyed: the body sagging, both front doors flung open, black smoke from the bonnet, the bumper bent. It stays on the ground for 6 s. When the player destroys it at close range, three soldiers jump out of the doors in game; they use the soldier's own sheet, so do not draw them.
- **Walk board, 8 × 4 = driving:** the wheels' spokes or hubcaps at four phases of a turn, and the body bobbing 1 px between frames. Keep the headlamps lit in all four. The game also plays it backwards for reversing, so do not draw exhaust or dust that only reads in one direction.
