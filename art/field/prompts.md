## Enemy atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE production game enemy sprite atlas for a Godot 2.5D pixel ARPG. Reference image 1 is STYLE ONLY: match the existing Lappland sprite's chibi proportions, blocky visible pixels, near-black outlines, restrained grey palette, readable light face/mask, hand-drawn 48-pixel game sprite aesthetic. Do not draw Lappland. Images 2-5 are PRTS enemy visual references (hooded masked raider, crossbowman, black spiky orange-core originium slug, white-fur icefield rifle hunter). Redraw as matching small pixel sprites. Output one square 1536x1536 RGBA image, genuinely transparent background, STRICT 3 columns x 3 rows of equal 512x512 cells, exactly one isolated full-body enemy centered in each cell, generous empty transparent padding, feet baseline at 88 percent of each cell height. All face three-quarter toward the viewer's left. No floor tiles, no backgrounds, no text, no labels, no cell borders, no watermarks. Each sprite is discrete crisp pixel art about 48x48 logical pixels enlarged, limited colors, no blur. Row 1 mine: column 1 dark grey hooded masked raider with red armband and improvised cleaver; column 2 ochre hooded white-mask crossbowman; column 3 squat black faceted spiky originium slug with bright amber underside. Row 2 urban ruins: column 1 charcoal street raider with short knife and rust-red scarf; column 2 charcoal hooded crossbowman with olive straps; column 3 bulky white coat and black visor heavy chainsaw fighter, red shoulder trim. Row 3 frozen forest: column 1 white fur hooded axe scout with small dark red scarf; column 2 white wide-brim hat and fur collar icefield rifle hunter from last reference; column 3 tall weathered split-bark wooden warrior with branch shoulders, dark hollow mask, cold muted turquoise fissures. Keep all humanoids similar chibi height except heavy enemies 15 percent bulkier. Preserve sharp silhouette readability and individual weapons. Return the atlas as a reusable transparent game asset.

## Environment props

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE reusable transparent environment prop atlas for a Godot 2.5D pixel-art ARPG. Image 1 is the existing Lappland player sprite, STYLE reference only: crisp visible square pixels, black/dark outlines, limited muted colors, chibi-scale environmental pixel art. Images 2-4 are PRTS Arknights map references for industrial Chernobog rubble, Lungmen urban concrete/metal streets, and Sami cold snowy forest; borrow their materials and atmosphere, NOT their tower-defense grid or UI icons. Output square 1536x1536 RGBA, real transparent background. STRICT 4 columns x 3 rows, 12 equal rectangular cells 384x512. Exactly ONE isolated full object per cell centered with transparent margins, no touching neighboring cells. Feet/base on a consistent baseline at 88 percent cell height. Camera: slight overhead three-quarter orthographic, sprites intended to stand upright as billboards beside a chibi character. Discrete pixel art approximately 64 logical pixels per object, strong dark contours, chunky pixel clusters, restrained texture, no gradients or blur. Row1 Chernobog mine: col1 rough dark rock outcrop with embedded amber black originium crystals; col2 broken timber mine support arch with rust metal braces and hanging warm lantern; col3 rusty mine cart filled with dark ore; col4 dented olive industrial supply crates and small hazard-striped canister as one compact pile. Row2 Lungmen: col1 damaged two-story narrow grey-blue concrete shop facade, shuttered lower storefront, ducts and a muted teal sign with NO letters; col2 battered waist-high grey concrete road barricade with yellow-black paint; col3 ruined compact dark sedan with cracked windows; col4 chipped concrete rubble with bent rebar. Row3 Sami: col1 snow-covered dark evergreen pine tree; col2 bare crooked dead tree with snow on branches; col3 rugged grey boulder covered by a thick snow cap; col4 small abandoned wooden shelter with steep snow-covered roof and black doorway. Do not include characters, shadows stretching outside object, text, symbols, cell frames, background rectangles, ground tiles, sky, or scenic backgrounds. Keep ground-contact shadows tiny and transparent. Maintain the reference character's hand-pixeled game style, not smooth concept painting.

## Ground atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. ONE game ground-material atlas, wide 1536x512 pixels divided into exactly THREE equal 512x512 square panels horizontally with NO margins, no separators. Orthographic straight top-down material textures for a chibi pixel-art ARPG matching the attached Lappland pixel sprite. Deliberately chunky pixel art, limited palettes, hand-placed pixel clusters, subdued low contrast so characters and red combat warning areas stand out. Each panel should independently tile seamlessly on all four sides. LEFT: Chernobog mine floor, warm charcoal grey packed gravel, broken grey stone slabs, occasional small ochre dust and rust flecks, sparse fine cracks. CENTER: Lungmen abandoned street, cool desaturated slate grey asphalt and irregular cracked concrete paving, very subtle grime, tiny scattered paper fragments; no lane lines, no painted symbols. RIGHT: Sami frozen forest floor, blue-grey weathered ice and dirty pale snow patches, tiny sparse dark pine needles and fine ice cracks, muted low contrast. Fill each entire square with flat ground. No perspective, no bevel borders, no raised objects, no terrain edges, no characters, no trees, no props, no lights, no grids, no text, no UI. Ground textures only. Opaque image.
