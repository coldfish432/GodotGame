# MEDICAL generated originals

These eight transparent PNGs are the image-generation originals. The local
`../build_medical.py` uniformly reduces and palette-quantizes them; per-item
integer factors and SHA-256 hashes are in `../manifest.json`.

Art direction: clean 3/4 Rhodes Island white-blue clinical furniture,
with `icu_2021`, the accepted console v4 and H2 hardware as references.
Separate originals cover the recovery bed, secondary bed, suspended vitals
monitor, folded privacy curtain, medicine cabinet, wash sink, IV stand and
sanitizer. Each piece has top/front/right depth and material shading. EKG
traces, accepted S mark and restrained blue fabric accents were polished
after uniform reduction on the native grid.
