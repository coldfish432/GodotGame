# 敌人移动原画生成提示词

生成方式：内置 imagegen；透明背景。参考本项目 enemies_v1.png 和对应敌人的八方向攻击原画。

## 暴徒

Use case: identity-preserve. Draw a NEW WALK-CYCLE ORIGINAL ART BOARD in the same crisp chunky pixel art style as these references. Image 1 is the original character identity; Image 2 is the character's direction/attack design reference. Use the same clothes, body proportions, colors and weapon. DO NOT copy attack actions: this board is WALKING locomotion only, relaxed carry posture.
Landscape, EXACTLY EIGHT COLUMNS x FOUR ROWS = 32 full-body walking keyframes. Large transparent empty gutters, complete figures and weapons inside every cell. Genuine zero-alpha transparent background, NO smoke/gradient/checkerboard/backdrop/ground shadows. Add column labels at top: S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE. Row labels at left: CONTACT A, PASS A, CONTACT B, PASS B.
Each column depicts one FOUR-FRAME looping walk:
Row1 CONTACT A = left leg reaches forward and right leg back, feet clearly apart, right arm counter-swings.
Row2 PASS A = right leg lifts and passes underneath body, left leg supports, body rises slightly.
Row3 CONTACT B = right leg reaches forward and left leg back, feet apart, opposite arm counter-swing.
Row4 PASS B = left leg lifts/passes, right leg supports, pose connects smoothly back to CONTACT A.
Clear alternating leg silhouettes, not four duplicates or mere translations. Keep head/torso scale fixed and foot ground line consistent; max small natural vertical body bob, coat/scarf follows step. Weapons remain securely held at low carry, no striking, aiming or firing. Front and back views MUST show alternating left/right knees and feet, not static standing poses.
EIGHT DISTINCT orientations across columns:
S direct FRONT toward viewer, both shoulders equal.
SW front-left diagonal toward bottom LEFT.
W pure LEFT profile, head and lead foot point LEFT.
NW BACK-left diagonal toward upper LEFT, back of clothing visible.
N direct BACK away/upward, NO FACE or front chest, both shoulders equal.
NE BACK-right diagonal toward upper RIGHT.
E pure RIGHT profile.
SE front-right diagonal toward bottom RIGHT.
All FOUR figures in a column face THAT direction. Never fill left-facing columns with right-facing sprites, never fill back columns with frontal sprites. Fixed camera slightly elevated top-down game perspective, not rotating the camera. Maintain original dark pixel outlines, clear clustered shading, chibi proportions. Entire artwork is only the 32 sprites and small labels. No scenery, effects, charge bars, arrows or projectiles.

Character: ONLY reference image 1 TOP LEFT and image 2 ROW 1: dark charcoal hood, shadowed upper face, white lower mask, white inner shirt, gray-black jacket/trousers/boots, red armband with white mark, battered single broad CLEAVER. NO axes, guns, dual swords or extra characters.
